My Daily Routine

Every morning, I usually wake up at 6:30 a.m. The first thing I do is check my phone and listen to some music while I get ready for the day. After that, I eat breakfast with my family. I normally drink coffee and eat eggs or toast.

Later, I go to school and spend most of the day in class. During breaks, I talk with my friends or buy snacks. When I return home, I do my homework and sometimes watch videos or play games to relax.

At night, I like spending some time on my hobbies before going to sleep. Even though my routine is simple, I enjoy the small moments of every day.
