Relaxing After School

Every evening after school, I like to spend some time relaxing at home. First, I change my clothes and eat a small snack because I am usually very hungry. Then, I sit on the couch and watch videos on my phone for a few minutes.

After relaxing, I start doing my homework or studying for exams. Sometimes it takes a lot of time, especially when I have difficult subjects. If I finish early, I play video games or talk with my friends online.

Before going to bed, I prepare my backpack and choose my clothes for the next day. This helps me feel calmer in the morning because I do not have to do everything quickly.
