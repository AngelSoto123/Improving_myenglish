Improving My Habits

Last year, I decided to change some habits in my life. I started waking up earlier, exercising three times a week, and spending less time on social media. At first, it was difficult because I was used to staying up late and wasting a lot of time online. However, after a few weeks, I noticed that I had more energy and felt more focused during the day.

One of the biggest changes was reading more books in English. This helped me improve my vocabulary and understand new expressions. I also began listening to podcasts and watching videos without subtitles. Sometimes I did not understand everything, but I continued practicing every day.

Now, I feel more confident when I speak English and I am motivated to keep improving. I learned that small changes can make a big difference if you stay consistent and patient.
